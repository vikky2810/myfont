Where it works

Notes

Titles, margins, little reminders to yourself. It reads like paper.

Signatures

Sign off a letter, a poster, a portfolio, without scanning anything.

Headlines

Big and loose. Pair it with a plain sans for anything long.

What it can and cannot do

Has

A to Z, a to z, and the marks: ! " ' , . : ; ?

Missing

Digits, hyphen, parentheses, slash, @ and &. They fall back to a plain sans.

Fix

Draw the missing tiles in Calligraphr and export again.

How to install it

Windows: right click the otf file, then Install. Mac: double click it, then Add Font. Web: drop the woff2 next to your CSS and point an @font-face rule at it.
